### Phase 1: The Industrial Challenge & Studio Overview (0:00 - 0:45)
- **Visual:** Screen recording of **FactoryVerse Studio** desktop browser interface (`https://localhost:8081`).
- **Narrator (VO):**
  > "Manufacturing facilities lose millions each year due to assembly defects and slow technician onboarding. Today, we present FactoryVerse Expert XR — an industrial work instruction and spatial training platform built on Meta IWSDK and WebXR, bringing PTC Vuforia Expert Capture capabilities directly to the open web and Meta Quest."
- **Actions on Screen:**
  1. Show **DashboardView**: Point out the 88.5% first-time pass rate and active SOPs.
  2. Navigate to **Expert Capture Workspace**: Turn on webcam/mic recording. Show the live waveform, click "Capture Step", and click "Convert to SOP Draft".
  3. Navigate to **Visual Procedure Editor**: Show the 3-column layout. Demonstrate reordering steps, inspecting the 3D preview, and configuring the 24.00 ± 0.10 mm tolerance gate and PPE safety checklist.

### Phase 2: 3D CAD Library & Exploded View (0:45 - 1:10)
- **Visual:** Switch to **3D Model Library**.
- **Narrator (VO):**
  > "FactoryVerse connects procedures directly to 3D CAD assets. In the Model Library, engineers can inspect internal components, drag-and-drop custom GLTF files, and toggle an interactive exploded view to understand how parts fit together before heading to the shop floor."
- **Actions on Screen:**
  1. Click through the component tree (Barrel, Piston Rod, Seal, Gland End Cap).
  2. Toggle "Exploded Assembly View" to animate parts expanding along their mechanical axis.

### Phase 3: Immersive Meta Quest WebXR Training (1:10 - 2:25)
- **Visual:** Transition into Meta Quest 3 WebXR view (first-person perspective).
- **Narrator (VO):**
  > "Now let's step into the virtual factory floor on Meta Quest. With Meta IWSDK, FactoryVerse provides a seated, hands-first training workstation with zero controller requirement."
- **Actions on Screen:**
  1. Show the operator sitting in front of the heavy industrial workbench with the hydraulic cylinder fixture.
  2. Point out the floating **Spatial UIKitML Instruction Panel** on the left and the animated **3D Spatial Wayfinding Beacon** hovering over the parts tray.
  3. **Hands-First Interaction:** Use natural hands to pinch-grab the hard-chrome piston rod. Bring it near the cylinder barrel bore — it magnetically snaps into place with a satisfying alignment!
  4. The Spatial Beacon moves automatically to hover over the brass gland end cap. Pinch and snap the end cap onto the barrel spigot.
  5. The Spatial Inspection Panel updates: Step 4 calls for measuring the datum seating gap.
  6. **Voice Commands:** Speak *"Pass"* into the microphone. The voice recognition engine transcribes the command, submits the 24.02 mm nominal measurement, and marks the dimensional check PASSED.
  7. The final **Training Score Report Panel** appears, awarding a 95/100 score with the first-time quality bonus.
